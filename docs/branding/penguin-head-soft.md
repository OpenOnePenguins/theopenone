Revised the integrated head-only logo with rounder cheeks and chin, soft charcoal instead of near-black, a warm gray face instead of bright white, and a muted yellow beak. The large eyes and existing placements remain.

Canonical asset: [open-one-logo.png](../../Sources/OpenOne/Assets/open-one-logo.png). Previous version: [open-one-head-v1.png](open-one-head-v1.png).

Generated using the built-in image generation tool. The second pass cleaned the transparent edge and restored a continuous charcoal chin rim.

Validation: refreshed the [UI preview](ui-preview.png) with the real toolbar, Settings, empty Talk, and light/dark menu samples. Rebuilt the app and icon, verified the code signature and bundled image, and checked the transparent exterior. Interior alpha is approximately 254/255; the softer contrast comes from the palette rather than materially reducing opacity.

## Initial edit prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit.
Refine this existing head-only penguin logo for The Open One. Two targeted changes: make the head a little rounder and soften the tonal contrast.
Shape: keep the same friendly penguin identity, big oval eyes, heart-shaped face patch and small yellow beak. Round the lower cheeks and chin into a smooth plump oval instead of the current broad flat bottom and slightly triangular silhouette. Make the head slightly narrower and fuller vertically, with width about 1.1 times height. Keep the crown smoothly domed. Do not turn it into a perfect circle or add ears. Maintain the large eyes and their friendly expression. Head only, no neck, body, flippers or feet.
Color: replace the nearly black head and eyes with a soft charcoal gray approximately #50545A. Replace the bright white face with a muted light warm gray approximately #D7D6D2. Keep the beak yellow but soften it to a gentle buttery golden yellow approximately #E8BD59. These are solid flat opaque colors, NOT reduced layer opacity. Keep enough separation for the facial features to be readable at 20 pixels while noticeably reducing the stark black/white contrast. No outlines, gradients, shading, texture, gloss, shadows or 3D.
Composition: centered isolated head on a square genuinely transparent background, occupying about 80 percent of canvas width and about 73 percent of canvas height; equal visual padding. Same clean professional minimal logo style, cute and calm, suitable for the existing dark glass native macOS toolbar and light menu bars. No background tile, no text, no extra elements.
