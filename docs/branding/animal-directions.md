## Exact generation prompts

### penguin

Use case: logo-brand.
Create an original animal mascot logo option for "The Open One", an open-source macOS companion for coding agents.
Input reference: previous cloud mascot, STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Preserve its extremely simple black-and-white visual language, plump soft silhouette, small closely spaced friendly eyes, and welcoming personality. Replace the cloud with the specified animal. Original design, not an existing project's mascot.
Style: flat vector-like logo, uniform near-black and white only, beautifully balanced smooth curves, minimal details, cute and professional. Broad sturdy shapes readable at 24 pixels. One centered compact mascot occupying about 70 percent of a square canvas, with generous transparent clearspace. No lettering, no words, no label, no watermark, no gradients, no shading, no texture, no 3D, no background tile, no detached decorative symbols, no outlines around the whole mascot. No giant staring eyes, no threatening expression, no sharp claws, no spooky ghost. Genuinely transparent background.
Subject: a very cute tiny plump penguin, front-facing with a slightly wider-than-tall bean-shaped body, miniature rounded feet and two short rounded flippers gently welcoming outward. Near-black body, one clean white face-and-belly patch, two small black dot eyes close together, a tiny rounded black beak. Make the overall head/body as continuous and simple as a cloud. A small transparent separation between right flipper and body subtly suggests an opening. Charming open-source community mascot. No resemblance to the exact pose or detailed rendering of Tux; create an original minimal penguin.
